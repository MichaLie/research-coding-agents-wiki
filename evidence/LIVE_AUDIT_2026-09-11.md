## Coverage and method

All 60 baseline entries were appraised. The final catalog contains 59 retained
entries and two additions, Pi and Zencoder: **61 displayed tools**. All final
public summaries carry `verified: 2026-09-11`; all 59 retained entries materially
changed. Nobie's prior row, stable ID and August verification date are preserved
in a reversible evidence hold rather than marked discontinued.

Verification means the dated public evidence summary was checked. It does not
certify a customer's private contract, institutional approval, runtime behavior
or every possible model/integration. Unsupported ancillary model inventories,
discounts, certification scope and precise prices were removed or qualified
before the final review. Inaccessible hosted terms remain visible limits, not
silently inferred guarantees. HTTP reachability alone was not treated as review.

Primary product, model/deployment, pricing, privacy/DPA/security, licensing and
maintenance pages were read across IDE/editor, terminal/CLI, cloud/autonomous,
local/self-hosted and data/notebook lanes. The integration pass checked notes,
tier gates, personal/special suitability and structured routes together.

## Material judgments

- **Kiro IDE/CLI:** Enterprise excludes service improvement but can store service
  content for operation. Individual paid accounts are not Enterprise. Regional
  processing, model abuse retention and administrator reporting remain separate.
- **Replit:** the September Enterprise Agreement explicitly applies to Enterprise;
  Core/Pro payment alone does not establish that no-training commitment.
- **Amp:** current Minimal Data Retention has provider/model exceptions and does
  not remove stored product threads or orb state. The provider limits and product
  deletion lifecycle are separately described.
- **Ante:** published SDK/protocol Apache licensing does not license its privately
  developed core binary. Current telemetry can export unscrubbed application logs;
  explicit exporter disable and local-log governance are required for the local route.
- **Clusy:** own-service no-training is now documented; provider controls remain
  non-uniform and some archives have indefinite retention. Personal and special
  recommendations remain `no`.
- **Zerve:** platform self-hosting does not prove in-boundary inference. Documented
  BYOK providers are OpenAI/Anthropic; self-hosted models are request-only. Special
  suitability is `no`; internally conflicting prices/credits are not resolved by
  choosing one number.
- **Roomote:** self-hosted task execution is documented, but an actual local model
  configuration was not established. Special suitability is therefore `no`.
- **Pi:** maintained MIT CLI with explicit local endpoints, default-on install
  telemetry, default-off analytics and persistent local sessions. Its conditional
  route disables telemetry/sharing and governs extensions, history and tool egress.
- **Zencoder:** private-deployment and custom-model docs explicitly describe
  customer-operated agents, orchestration and inference. Free-account training,
  paid/trial consent and provider/product retention are separately stated; the
  default cloud product is not presented as zero-storage.
- **DataFoundry:** parent documentation names the same product and directs users
  to the existing distribution repository. Matching README/package/architecture/
  security documents are evidence of that relationship, not proof that every code
  file is identical. One existing ID is retained for its governed local SQL use;
  early maturity and roadmap status remain explicit.
- **Nobie:** current repository describes an engine for external coding agents.
  Its own agent/model boundary and distinct researcher privacy need were not
  established. The snapshot and exact reconsideration conditions are preserved.

Orion remains a discovery hold: current releases and Apache license are visible,
but required cloud sign-in and publication/storage complicate its local-first
positioning. Other discovery-only orchestration layers, thin/early candidates
and general platforms were not admitted without distinctive research fit and
adequate product evidence. Existing omissions were not treated as permanent bans.

## Data and presentation semantics

The additive schema value `data_handling: unknown` distinguishes unestablished
training/retention from known training permission. Zerve receives the `Unclear`
label; Clusy receives a scoped `no-train` label. No privacy suitability rule was
weakened: **0 special-ready, 33 configuration-required, 28 not appropriate**.
Local, ZDR and No-train legends now describe route/plan scope rather than blanket
guarantees. The version badge describes catalog metadata, not publication status.

Read-only inspection of Navigator and Trial Agent consumers found string/display/
count handling rather than a closed data-handling enum. Those projects were not
modified or refreshed by this work.
